# Privacy by Design Policy

OffLoop AI is built from the ground up to respect user privacy and eliminate unnecessary data harvesting:

1. **Local-First Architecture:** All journal entries, reflections, captured photos, and completed quest logs are stored exclusively in your device's local Room database.
2. **Zero Breadcrumb GPS Tracking:** The application only queries the immediate current coordinate when searching for nearby public places and evaluating arrival proximity. Continuous movement breadcrumbs are never recorded, tracked, or transmitted to any server.
3. **No Mandatory Accounts:** You can explore, generate quests, unlock badges, and track wellbeing without creating an account or providing an email address.
4. **Local Photo Protection:** Photos captured during optional observation challenges remain on your local device storage. They are never uploaded or shared without explicit user action.
5. **No Invasive Permissions:** The app never requests access to your contacts, SMS, call logs, or personal media libraries.
